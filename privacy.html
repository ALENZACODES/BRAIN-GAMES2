Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version

  • You don’t need an account. We don’t ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • Your progress is saved in your own browser, on your own device. We can’t see it.
  • If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage stats (what you play, scores, timings, taps, device type) so we can fix and improve the games. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Online matches connect your device directly to your friend’s device.
  • No ads. We never sell your information.

1. Who we are

Prismind (“Prismind”, “we”, “us”) is a set of puzzle games operated by ALENZA CODES, Ontario, Canada. Questions about this policy: .

2. Information that stays on your device

The app saves your progress in your browser’s local storage: scores, levels, stars, badges, streaks, missions, settings, your rivals’ display names and match records, and the display name you choose. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it unless you share it (for example, by sending a challenge link). Using “Delete all progress” in Settings erases it. Clearing your browser data also erases it.

3. Anonymous usage stats (only if you allow it)

When you first open the app, we ask whether you want to share anonymous usage stats. If you choose “Allow”, the app sends the following to our analytics provider:

  • A random ID created on your device, so we can tell how many people come back. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Gameplay: games opened and played, scores, levels, answers marked right or wrong, response times, streaks, stars, badges, missions, matches, quits and errors.
  • App use: screens viewed, buttons and links tapped, settings changed, whether the app is installed, how long sessions last, and how you arrived (for example, from a shared link).
  • Device information: device and browser type, operating system, screen size, language and time zone.
  • Approximate location: our provider receives your IP address as part of the connection and uses it to estimate your country and region.
  • Session recordings may be collected: a replay of what was shown on screen and where you tapped. Text you type is masked.

We do not send your display name or your rivals’ names in usage stats.

Why: to find bugs, see which games are too easy or too hard, understand which features people use, and measure how many players return.

Provider: PostHog, Inc. processes these stats for us. Its servers are in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed outside Canada and may be accessible to authorities under the laws of that country.

How long: we keep usage stats for up to 24 months, then delete them.

Your choice: you can turn usage stats on or off at any time in Settings → Privacy. If you choose “No thanks”, nothing is sent.

4. Online matches

When you play a friend online, the app uses PeerJS, an open-source connection service, to introduce the two devices using a 5-letter room code. The devices then connect directly to each other. If a direct connection isn’t possible, traffic may pass through a relay server run by PeerJS. During a match, the devices exchange your display name, the game chosen, and your live score, level and lives.

As with any internet connection, your friend’s device and the PeerJS servers can see your IP address. We do not store match messages. Only share room codes with people you know.

5. Links you share

Challenge and invite links contain what you choose to share: your display name, the game, your score and a room code. Anyone who receives the link can see this.

6. Cookies and local storage

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser’s local storage to save your progress. If you allow usage stats, it also uses local storage to hold the random analytics ID.

7. Children

Prismind is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian who believes their child has shared information with us can contact us, and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access or delete the usage stats linked to your anonymous ID, or withdraw your consent. Your anonymous ID is shown in Settings → Privacy. Send it to us at the contact above. We will respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the data-protection authority where you live.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Usage stats are sent over encrypted connections. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we limit what we collect to what is described here.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If we start collecting new kinds of information, we will ask for your permission again first.
